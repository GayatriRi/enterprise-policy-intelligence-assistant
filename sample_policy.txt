Employee Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 15 days of paid annual leave per year.

Employees may also take up to 5 days of paid personal leave for family or personal reasons.

All leave requests should be submitted through the HR portal and approved by the employee's manager.

Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

Remote work may be allowed up to two days per week with manager approval.
